🖥️ DESKTOP CONTROLS

  • Look Around: Move mouse cursor toward screen edges
  • Blast Doors: Click red wall buttons
  • Doorway Lights: Click white wall buttons
  • Camera Monitor: Press SPACE / S / ESC or hover over bottom bar
  • Switch Cameras: Click buttons on minimap (CAM 1A - 7)

📱 MOBILE CONTROLS

  • Look Around: Swipe / drag finger left or right across the screen
  • Doors & Lights: Tap red and white wall buttons directly
  • Camera Monitor: Tap bottom bar ("CAMERA MONITOR")

HOW TO PLAY ON PHONE

If you play inside your browser, the giant Safari/Chrome address bars will block the doors, lights, and camera buttons!

🍏 iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. 1
    Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (⎋ square with arrow pointing UP).
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).
  3. 3
    Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
  4. 4
    Go to your phone's Home Screen, tap "FNAF Friends", turn your phone sideways, and enjoy TRUE fullscreen with NO BARS!
🤖 Android (Chrome)
  1. 1
    Tap the Three Dots (⋮) menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  2. 2
    Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  3. 3
    Tap "Install", then open the app icon from your Home Screen!
